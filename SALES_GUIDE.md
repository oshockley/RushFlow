## Sales Philosophy

### The Golden Rules

**Rule 1: Sell Outcomes, Not Features**
- ❌ "We use AI voice recognition technology"
- ✅ "You'll serve 20 more customers per hour"

**Rule 2: Make Them Feel the Pain First**
- Don't lead with your solution
- Ask questions that make them realize they have a problem
- *"How long does it take to enter a complex order during rush hour?"*

**Rule 3: Show, Don't Tell**
- A 90-second demo > 30-minute presentation
- Let them hold the phone and try it

**Rule 4: ROI Over Everything**
- Every conversation ends with: *"That's $3,950 more profit per month for $150"*

**Rule 5: Start with the Easiest Customers**
- Independent coffee shops (Month 1-2)
- Then juice bars and food trucks (Month 3-4)
- Then QSR franchises (Month 5-6)
- Save enterprise for Month 6+ when you have case studies

## Target Customer Profiles

### ICP #1: Independent Coffee Shop (EASIEST)
**Profile:**
- 2-10 employees
- $300k-2M annual revenue
- Gets slammed 7-9am weekdays
- Owner works behind counter
- Uses Square or Toast POS

**Pain Points:**
- Long lines during rush → customers leave
- Staff stressed and making mistakes
- Can't afford to hire more people
- Losing revenue every busy morning

**Your Angle:**
- Show them the app during slow afternoon hours (2-4pm)
- Offer free trial starting tomorrow morning
- ROI pitch: "Serve 20 more customers = $137/day = $4,100/month extra"

**Where to Find Them:**
- Google Maps: "coffee shop near me"
- Instagram: Local coffee shop hashtags
- Yelp: Read reviews mentioning "long wait times"

**Average Deal Size:** $150/month, 6-month contracts

---

### ICP #2: Juice Bar / Smoothie Shop
**Profile:**
- Located in gyms, shopping centers, college campuses
- Complex orders with lots of add-ins and substitutions
- Health-conscious customers (often on the phone ordering)
- Uses various POS systems

**Pain Points:**
- Orders have 5-10 modifications each
- Takes 45 seconds to input one smoothie order
- Peak times: Pre-work (7-8am), post-workout (5-7pm)
- High staff turnover → constant retraining on POS

**Your Angle:**
- Demo with complex order: "Acai bowl, no banana, add hemp seeds, almond butter, extra blueberries"
- Show how it handles modifiers perfectly
- ROI: Same $4k/month calculation

**Where to Find Them:**
- Partner with gym chains
- College campus juice bars
- Shopping mall food courts

**Average Deal Size:** $150-200/month

---

### ICP #3: Food Trucks
**Profile:**
- Limited space, mobile operation
- Long lines at lunch (11am-2pm) and events
- Often one owner + one employee
- Needs fast turnover to maximize event opportunities

**Pain Points:**
- Can't serve fast enough at busy events
- People leave lines when it's too slow
- Miss out on event revenue potential
- iPad POS is clunky in small truck space

**Your Angle:**
- Pitch at weekend farmer's markets or food truck parks
- "Serve 30% more customers at your next festival"
- Offer to test at their next event for free

**Where to Find Them:**
- Food truck parks on weekends
- Instagram: #foodtruck + your city
- Food truck associations/groups

**Average Deal Size:** $125-150/month (negotiate on volume)

---

### ICP #4: Quick Service Restaurants (QSR) - Franchises
**Profile:**
- Subway, Panda Express, Jimmy John's, etc.
- Corporate POS systems (harder integration)
- Multiple locations = bigger deal
- Decision-maker is franchise owner (not staff)

**Pain Points:**
- Labor shortage → constantly understaffed
- Corporate pressure on speed metrics (time per order)
- Compliance with corporate POS systems
- Training new employees takes weeks

**Your Angle:**
- Need enterprise features (multi-location dashboard)
- May need corporate approval
- Start with single location, prove ROI, then expand
- Pitch: "Save 40 hours/month on order entry across all locations"

**Where to Find Them:**
- LinkedIn: Search "franchise owner + [your city]"
- Franchise owner associations
- Multi-unit owner networking groups

**Average Deal Size:** $500-1,500/month (3-10 locations)

## Lead Generation Strategies

**Strategy 1: Walk-In Method (Best for Coffee Shops)**
1. Visit during slow hours (2-4pm)
2. Buy a coffee first (builds rapport)
3. Ask to speak with owner/manager
4. Pitch: "I built something that might help with your morning rush. Got 2 minutes?"

**Expected Results:**
- 10 visits = 6 conversations = 2 demos = 1 interested lead
- Do 5 visits per day = 1-2 leads per day

```
You: "Hey! This cold brew is amazing. Is the owner around?"
Staff: "Yeah, that's me."

Owner: "Oh yeah, the morning rush is crazy."
```

## Demo Script (The Perfect Pitch)

### Preparation (Before You Arrive)
- [ ] Charge phone to 100%
- [ ] Test app on data connection (not wifi)
- [ ] Prepare 3 example orders matching their menu
- [ ] Have pricing sheet ready (1-page PDF)
- [ ] Bring business cards

### The 5-Minute Demo

**Minute 1: Build Rapport**
```
"Thanks for taking the time! Love [specific detail about their shop]. 
How long have you been running this place?"

[Let them talk for 30-60 seconds]
```

**Minute 2: Identify Pain**
```
"So during morning rush - what's your biggest bottleneck? Making drinks 
or taking orders?"

[They'll usually say "taking orders"]

"Right. And when you get a super complex order - large iced oat milk 
latte, extra shot, no whip, light ice - how long does that take to 
punch into your POS?"

[They'll say 20-40 seconds]

"Yeah, adds up fast. 200 orders × 30 seconds = 1.5 hours of just 
typing instead of serving customers."
```

**Minute 3: Show the Demo**
```
"Let me show you what we built. [Open app]

I'm just going to speak naturally like a customer would:
'Large iced latte with oat milk and an extra shot'

[Show the transcription and parsed order in 5 seconds]

See how it caught everything? All the modifiers, the size, milk preference, 
everything. Now this gets sent directly to your [Square/Toast/Clover] POS.

No training needed. Your staff just talks to it like they would to you."
```

**Minute 4: Show the ROI**
```
"So here's the math. [Pull out pricing sheet]

You're doing about 200 orders during peak hours, right?

Saving 25 seconds per order = 83 minutes saved
That's time to serve 21 more customers
21 customers × $6.50 average ticket = $137/day
$137 × 30 days = $4,100/month in extra revenue

Our cost is $150/month.

So net gain for you is $3,950/month.

And that's just during breakfast rush. Doesn't even count lunch."
```

**Minute 5: Handle Objections & Close**
```
"What do you think? Would this help during your busy hours?"

[Listen to their concerns - address them - see Objection Handling section]

"How about this - I'll set you up with a free 60-day trial. No credit 
card required. I'll come in tomorrow morning and train your staff in 
15 minutes.

After 60 days, if you're serving more customers and making more money, 
we go live at $150/month. If not, no hard feelings.

Fair enough?"
```

---

### Demo Tips

**DO:**
- ✅ Use their menu items in your demo
- ✅ Let them hold the phone and try it
- ✅ Show a complex order with modifications
- ✅ Talk slowly and clearly (builds confidence)
- ✅ Ask questions throughout (keeps them engaged)

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Use technical jargon ("AI voice recognition", "natural language processing")
- ❌ Show them backend code or settings
- ❌ Talk about features they don't care about
- ❌ Rush through the demo
- ❌ Bad-mouth their current POS system

## Objection Handling

### Objection 1: "It's too expensive"

**Response:**
```
"I totally get that $150 feels like a lot. Let me ask you this - 

How much revenue do you do per month?"
[They say $50k-100k]

"Okay, so $150 is 0.15-0.3% of your revenue. 

But what we're seeing with other shops is they're doing $4,000-5,000 
more per month because they're serving more customers.

So it's not really an expense - it's an investment that pays for itself 
26 times over.

Would you spend $1 to make $26?"
```

**Alternative Response:**
```
"What if we did $99/month for the first 3 months? That way you can test 
it during your busiest season and see the ROI before committing to full price."
```

---

### Objection 2: "We don't have time to learn new technology"

**Response:**
```
"Great news - there's nothing to learn. 

Your staff already knows how to talk, right? That's it. That's the whole training.

I'll come in tomorrow morning and spend 15 minutes showing your team. 
By 8:15am they'll be using it during rush.

And if they have any questions, I'm one text message away for the first 30 days.

The shops using it say it's actually easier than their POS because they 
don't have to memorize where all the buttons are."
```

---

### Objection 3: "What if it makes mistakes?"

**Response:**
```
"That's a smart question. Here's how it works:

The app shows them the order on screen before sending it to your POS. 
So if it misses something, they can just fix it with one tap.

We're seeing 95-98% accuracy, which is actually better than humans typing 
on the POS during rush hours when they're stressed.

And every time there's a correction, it learns and gets better.

But I'll tell you what - during your free trial, we track every single 
order. After 30 days, I'll show you the accuracy data. If it's not saving 
you time, we part ways. Deal?"
```

---

### Objection 4: "We need to think about it"

**Response:**
```
"Totally fair! This is a business decision.

Can I ask what specifically you need to think about? Is it the price, 
the timing, or you're not sure it'll work for your menu?"

[Let them tell you the real objection]

"Got it. How about this - I'll leave you a guest login to the app so you 
can test it yourself over the next few days.

And I'll follow up on [specific day] to answer any questions. Sound good?"
```

**Alternative Close:**
```
"I hear you. Just so you know, I'm only taking on 5 beta customers this 
month, and I have 2 spots left.

If you want to reserve one of those spots for a free trial, I need to 
know by [date]. After that, we'll have a waitlist until next quarter.

No pressure, but I don't want you to miss out if this could help during 
your busy season."
```

---

### Objection 5: "Our POS already does this" or "We use XYZ feature"

**Response:**
```
"Oh nice! How long have you been using that feature?"
[They usually haven't actually used it or didn't know it existed]

"Here's the difference though - does it integrate directly with your 
POS, or is it a separate system you have to sync?"

[Most built-in features are clunky]

"What we built is native integration. Voice goes straight to your POS 
order ticket in real-time. No export/import, no second screen.

Want to see the difference? Takes 30 seconds."

[Show them your seamless demo]
```

---

### Objection 6: "We're already using [competitor]"

**Response:**
```
"Oh awesome! How's that working for you?"

[Listen carefully - they'll usually mention something they don't like]

"Yeah, I've heard that from other shops. The main things they switched 
to us for were:

1. Speed - ours is 2-3x faster because of how we process the AI
2. Accuracy - we specialize in food service, so modifiers are our strength
3. Support - I'm local and can come help in person if needed

How about I set you up with a side-by-side trial? Use both for 30 days 
and see which one your staff prefers?"
```

---

### Objection 7: "Let me talk to my business partner/manager"

**Response:**
```
"Absolutely! Are they here now? I can show both of you real quick."

[If they're not there:]

"No problem. Can I get their contact info? I'll send them a quick video 
showing how it works, then follow up with both of you."

[Or offer to come back when they're both there]

"When's a good time when you're both working? I'll come back and do the 
demo for both of you together - takes 5 minutes."
```

## Pricing & Closing

### Your Pricing Tiers

**Tier 1: Single Location ($150/month)**
- 1 terminal/device
- Unlimited voice orders
- Basic support (email)
- POS integration included
- Custom menu setup

**Tier 2: Small Chain ($450/month for 3-5 locations)**
- $150/location/month
- Multi-location dashboard
- Priority support (phone + email)
- Dedicated account manager
- Quarterly business reviews

**Tier 3: Enterprise ($1,500/month for 10+ locations)**
- $100-125/location/month (volume discount)
- Custom integrations
- 24/7 support
- On-site training
- API access
- White-label option

### Contract Terms

**Option A: Monthly (Recommended for First 20 Customers)**
- Month-to-month, cancel anytime
- Easier to close because lower commitment
- 30-day notice to cancel

**Option B: Annual (10% Discount)**
- $1,620/year ($135/month) instead of $1,800
- Upfront payment or quarterly installments
- Offer after they've been using for 2-3 months

**Option C: Multi-Year (20% Discount)**
- $1,440/year ($120/month)
- Only for 5+ location deals
- Shows long-term commitment

### Closing Techniques

**The Assumptive Close:**
```
"Great! So I'll get you set up with a free trial starting tomorrow. 
What time does your morning rush start? I'll come in 30 minutes before 
that to train your team."
```

**The Alternative Close:**
```
"Would you want to start the trial this week or next week?"
[Not "do you want to start" but "which week"]
```

**The Puppy Dog Close:**
```
"How about this - use it free for 60 days. After that, if your staff 
wants to keep it, we go live at $150/month. If they don't like it, 
no hard feelings. You're not going to fall in love with it?"
```

**The Takeaway Close:**
```
"You know what, I'm not sure this is the right fit for you. You mentioned 
you only do 100 orders during rush, and most of our successful customers 
are doing 200+. You might not see enough ROI to justify the $150.

Unless... do you think you could get to 200 orders if the line moved faster?"
```

**The Silence Close:**
```
[After presenting the offer]

"So what do you think?"

[SHUT UP AND WAIT. Don't fill the silence. First person to talk loses.]
```

## Scaling to $10k

### What Changes at Each Revenue Level

**$0-$1k MRR: Do Everything Yourself**
- You are sales, support, product, marketing
- Focus: Find product-market fit
- Work 60-80 hours/week

**$1k-$3k MRR: Hire Your First Contractor**
- Hire part-time SDR or customer success person
- Automate email follow-ups
- Build basic FAQ/help center
- Work 50-60 hours/week

**$3k-$6k MRR: Build a Small Team**
- Full-time SDR or salesperson
- Part-time developer for features
- Part-time customer success
- You focus on: Closing big deals, partnerships, strategy
- Work 40-50 hours/week

**$6k-$10k MRR: Scale Systems**
- 2 salespeople + 1 customer success
