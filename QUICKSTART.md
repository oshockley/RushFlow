# 🚀 Quick Start: Pitch-Ready Demo in 3 Minutes

## Step 1: Open the App (30 seconds)

```bash
npm start
# Then press 'w' for web, or scan QR with Expo Go app
```

## Step 2: Configure for Your Prospect (1 minute)

1. Tap the **⚙️ Settings** button (bottom right)
2. Fill in:
   - **Business Name:** `[Prospect's Coffee Shop Name]`
   - **Brand Color:** `[Their hex color, e.g., #8B4513]`
3. Select a menu template:
   - **☕ Coffee Shop** (lattes, cappuccinos)
   - **🍔 Quick Service** (burgers, fries)
4. Tap **"💾 Save Settings"**

## Step 3: Test the Demo (1 minute)

1. Go back to main screen (tap ← Back)
2. Tap the **blue record button**
3. Say: *"Large oat milk latte with an extra shot and a blueberry muffin"*
4. Tap **stop**
5. Watch AI process (2 seconds)
6. Review the parsed order
7. Tap **"✓ Confirm & Send to POS"**

## Done! ✅

You're now ready to demo to any coffee shop or restaurant!
