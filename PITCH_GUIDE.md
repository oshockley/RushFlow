# 🎯 Pitch Guide - How to Demo SpeechFlow AI to Businesses

## Quick Start Checklist

Before meeting with a prospect:
- [ ] Customize business name in Settings
- [ ] Set their brand color (look up on website)
- [ ] Load appropriate menu (coffee shop vs. restaurant)
- [ ] Test 2-3 voice orders
- [ ] Ensure Demo Mode is enabled
- [ ] Charge your device fully

---

## 📱 Pre-Meeting Setup (5 minutes)

### 1. Open Settings (tap ⚙️ icon)

### 2. Configure Business Info
```
Business Name: [Prospect's Business Name]
Brand Color: [Their hex color - or use preset]
Demo Mode: ✓ ON
```

### 3. Load Appropriate Menu
- **Coffee Shop?** → Load "☕ Coffee Shop Menu"
- **Restaurant/QSR?** → Load "🍔 Quick Service Menu"

### 4. Save Settings
Tap "💾 Save Settings" button

---

## 🎤 The Demo Script

### Opening (30 seconds)
**You:** "I want to show you something that could save your team 15-20 seconds per complex order. May I show you?"

**[Pull out tablet/phone]**

"This is SpeechFlow AI. Instead of tapping 10+ buttons for a complex drink order, your barista just speaks it."

### Demo Flow (2 minutes)

**Step 1: Record Order**
- Tap the blue record button
- Say clearly: *"Large oat milk latte with an extra shot and a blueberry muffin"*
- Tap stop

**Step 2: Show Processing**
- Point out "Processing order..." with spinner
- Explain: "Our AI is converting speech to text and parsing the order"

**Step 3: Review Screen**
- Show the voice transcription card
- Point to itemized order with all modifiers
- **Highlight the accuracy** - size, milk type, extras all captured
- Show the total price calculation

**Step 4: Confirm**
- Tap "✓ Confirm & Send to POS"
- Show success message with POS Order ID

### Key Points to Emphasize

1. **Speed**: "What took 30 seconds is now 5 seconds"
2. **Accuracy**: "See how it caught every modifier? Large, oat milk, extra shot"
3. **Easy Training**: "New staff learn in minutes instead of hours"
4. **No Hardware**: "Works on tablets you already have"

## 💬 Handling Common Questions

### "How accurate is it?"
**Answer:** "95%+ accuracy in our tests. And even in noisy cafés, it works well because we train specifically on coffee shop environments. Plus, there's always a review screen before it goes to your POS."

### "What if it gets something wrong?"
**Answer:** "Great question. See this review screen? Staff always confirms before sending. And there's an edit button if anything needs adjustment."

### "How does it integrate with our POS?"
**Answer:** "We have direct integrations with Square, Toast, Clover, and MICROS. [Which do you use?] It typically takes 2-3 hours to set up and we handle everything."

### "How much does it cost?"
**Answer:** "It's $150 per month per terminal. Think of it like this: that's $5 a day. If it saves you 15 seconds per order, and you do 300 orders per day, that's 75 minutes saved—more than an hour of labor cost. It pays for itself day one."

### "Do we need new hardware?"
**Answer:** "Nope! Works on iPads, Android tablets, or even smartphones you already have. Just needs iOS 13+ or Android 5+"

### "What about noisy environments?"
**Answer:** "Actually designed for it. Coffee grinders, steam wands, background music. We tested in real cafés. The noise-canceling tech handles it."

## 💰 Pricing Presentation

### Frame it as ROI:
```
Cost: $150/month per terminal
     = $5/day
     = Less than 1 hour of minimum wage labor

Savings: 15 seconds × 300 orders = 75 minutes/day
       = 12.5 hours/week  
       = 650 hours/year
       = $8,125/year saved (at $12.50/hour)

ROI: 450% return on investment
```

### Offer Structure:
1. **30-day free trial** - "No credit card needed"
2. **$150/month** - Standard pricing
3. **$500 setup fee** - Waived for first 10 customers
4. **Premium ($250/month)** - Includes analytics dashboard

## 🎯 Target Customers (Best Fit)

### Ideal First Customers:
- ✅ Independent coffee shops (3-5 locations)
- ✅ 300+ transactions per day
- ✅ Manager/owner is tech-forward
- ✅ Currently using Square/Toast/Clover
- ✅ Hiring challenges (can't find trained staff)

### Red Flags (Maybe Later):
- ❌ Single-owner shop with <100 orders/day
- ❌ Using ancient legacy POS with no API
- ❌ "We're happy with how things are"
- ❌ No tablets/devices available

3. **vs. Hiring More Staff:**
   - "Labor costs $2,500+/month"

## 🚀 After the Pitch

### If they say YES:
1. Schedule setup call within 48 hours
2. Send agreement/paperwork
3. Get their menu items (PDF or spreadsheet)
4. Schedule training session (30 min)

### If they say MAYBE:
1. Send recap email immediately
2. Offer to come back during peak hours to show real-time
3. Ask for referral: "Do you know other owners who might benefit?"
4. Follow up in 3 days

### If they say NO:
1. Ask: "What would make this a yes?"
2. Leave door open: "Can I check back in 3 months?"
3. Request referral anyway
4. Note objections for product improvement

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Demo during their peak hours** - They feel the pain most then
2. **Bring a second device** - Show order confirmation on "POS side"
3. **Record successful demos** - Use for marketing later
4. **Bring a mini speaker** - Better audio if showing to group
5. **Have backup: phone hotspot** - Though demo mode works offline
